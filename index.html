RP Themes

Is your character a Wayfinder?

A Wayfinder is someone who finds the path, whether that means guiding others through unknown territory or working out how to reach a goal. In a more figurative sense, a Wayfinder is a seeker: of forgotten knowledge, of roads no one has travelled, of answers to problems no one has solved, or of new approaches no one has thought to try.

Examples of Wayfinders you might find amongst our ranks:

  • The Scholarever seeking to expand their knowledge
  • The Relic Hunteralways looking for what was lost
  • The Druidlistening to Nature's call
  • The Exilesearching for a new place to call home
  • The Explorerdriven to chart what lies beyond the edge of the map
  • The Seerfollowing visions and omens toward a fate not yet written
  • The Tinkerconvinced there is always a better way to build it
  • The Smugglerknowing answers to problems you couldn't even think of

The Seeker's Trial

Three answers sealed and sent to the GM alone,
will earn you a rank few have known.

✦ Trial Completed ✦
Amarthan Everthorn
12/11/26

The answers have now been revealed.
Stay tuned: another trial awaits those who missed this one!

  1. Romance once budded here,
    same place we lost many near and dear.
    Serves a vintage so rare that few ever pour,
    except behind the High King's door.
    Answer Dalaran, where Jaina and Arthas' romance bloomed. King Anduin keeps bottles of the rare Peaked Dalaran Red in Stormwind Keep.
  2. Glory be his name,
    Brow of steel and Light aflame.
    Once had a brother, turned to twilight,
    we amongst the Legion had to fight.
    Answer Glorin Steelbrow, the colleague Bishop Farthing sends us to. Farthing was later revealed as the new leader of the Twilight's Hammer, and shadow priests defeated him in their Legion artifact questline.
  3. Axe bloodied with youth,
    for nothing this monster could soothe.
    Track him down we must,
    should no one else fall to his lust.
    Answer Stalvan Mistmantle, the twisted man we learn about in Duskwood who slaughtered young Tilloa and her groom-to-be with an axe.
